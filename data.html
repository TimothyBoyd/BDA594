Cleaning and Processing Procedures

311 Data

  • Removed irrelevant categories (non-encampment calls)
  • Standardized timestamps to UTC
  • Geocoded addresses
  • Removed duplicate entries
  • Cleaned text for topic modeling
  • Filtered dominant topics (FastTopic)

Policy Data

  • Extracted dates, jurisdictional scale (state/city/local), and type
  • Geocoded to nearest relevant administrative boundary
  • Organized into an interactive timeline using ARC Experience

Climate Data

  • Converted GHCN from daily → monthly summaries
  • Extracted ERA5 from netCDF and converted Kelvin → Fahrenheit
  • Standardized all climate variables with z-scores
  • Merged with homelessness time series for seasonal comparisons

Team Data Processing Summaries

Ashley: Policy Data Compilation & Mapping

Ashley compiled and verified homelessness-related policy actions, geocoded them into a spatial database, standardized categories, and created annual policy maps. She integrated these layers into an interactive ArcGIS Experience to show how sweeps, ordinances, and resource changes align with 311 hotspots and migration patterns.

Tim: 311 Topic Modeling & Spatial Hotspots

Tim cleaned and filtered 311 service requests, identified homelessness-related records, and applied FastTopic modeling to reveal major themes in resident concerns. He generated KDE hotspot maps and built an interactive temporal heatmap showing how public reporting patterns change across neighborhoods and months.

Jonathan: Climate Data Processing & Integration

Jonathan collected and processed daily and reanalysis climate data, aggregated weather metrics into monthly values, standardized variables, and aligned climate data with homelessness counts. His visualizations highlight how heat and rainfall influence the seasonal visibility of unsheltered populations.

Master Dataset Integration

A unified spatiotemporal schema was created in which:

  • Policies = point events
  • Weather = monthly time-series variables
  • 311 calls = heatmap inputs and topic-modeled text
