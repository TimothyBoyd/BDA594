Team roles and responsibilities.

Team Roles and Responsibilities

Ashley Funk

  • Collected, cleaned, and categorized policy data from 2019–2025
  • Created an interactive geocoded policy map with clickable timeline
  • Classified policies into sweeps, encampment bans, shelter expansions/closures, PITC-related actions, and citywide action plans
  • Worked on statistical model development and produced the group video
  • Prepared the website presentation of policy and geospatial layers

Timothy Boyd

  • Obtained 311 call data from the Get It Done website
  • Cleaned and processed raw call records
  • Created heatmaps of 311 homeless-related calls to identify persistent hotspots
  • Applied topic modeling to understand public sentiment toward homelessness

Jonathan Siegel

  • Collected and processed climate datasets including GHCN-Daily, ERA5-Land, and Downtown Homeless Sleepers data
  • Aggregated daily climate data into monthly averages and calculated key indicators such as maximum temperature, precipitation totals, and heat-day counts
  • Standardized climate and homelessness variables using z-scores to enable direct comparison across time
  • Merged climate metrics with monthly homelessness counts to analyze seasonal and environmental patterns
  • Created time-series visualizations showing relationships between heat, precipitation, and unsheltered population trends
  • Identified recurring seasonal patterns, including increased homelessness visibility during warm months and decreases during periods of heavy rainfall
  • Documented data limitations such as missing months, climate dataset size constraints, and challenges with poorly documented environmental datasets
