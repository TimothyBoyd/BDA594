Encampment Migration Summary

This page summarizes how encampment locations shifted over time in response to policy actions, community reporting patterns, and climate variability across San Diego.

Policy Impacts on Encampment Migration

Policy interventions—especially encampment sweeps and new regulations—were closely tied to movement of unsheltered populations. Sweeps in Downtown and Mid-City often pushed encampments into nearby neighborhoods, where 311 activity rose after enforcement. In contrast, new shelters and safe-parking resources sometimes stabilized nearby encampment presence.

311 Hotspot Patterns and Migration

Homelessness-related 311 reports revealed recurring migration corridors, particularly Downtown → Mid-City and Mission Bay → Pacific Beach. Topic-specific hotspots showed how different types of encampments shifted over time, often following enforcement or seasonal pressures.

Climate Influences on Encampment Movement

Climate variability played a measurable role in encampment mobility. Hotter months corresponded with increased outdoor encampment presence, while heavy rainfall reduced visible encampments as individuals sought shelter or moved to covered areas.
