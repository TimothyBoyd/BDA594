About · Weather, Policy, and Topic Modeling of 311 Reports on Homelessness in San Diego

About this project.

About

This project examines how environmental conditions, policy actions, and community reporting shape the visibility and movement of unsheltered populations in San Diego. Using 311 encampment reports, GIS spatial analysis, urban policy data, and NLP topic modeling, we aim to capture patterns that traditional homelessness counts often overlook.

Research Questions

  • Where are encampments most concentrated, and how does density vary across the city?
  • How do policy interventions relate to changes or displacement in encampment locations?
  • What themes appear in 311 complaint text, and how do these narratives align with spatial patterns?

By integrating spatial, policy, and textual data, this project connects GIS, urban policy, and topic modeling to better understand homelessness in San Diego.
