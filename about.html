About this project.

About

Homelessness in San Diego is shaped by environmental conditions, policy actions, and community reporting. Traditional methods like point-in-time counts miss daily shifts in where unsheltered people live. This project uses 311 encampment reports, GIS spatial analysis, urban policy data, and NLP topic modeling to better understand how policy and environment influence the visibility and movement of unhoused populations. We ask: Where are encampments most concentrated? How do policy interventions relate to changes or displacement? What themes appear in 311 complaint text, and how do these narratives align with spatial patterns? By integrating spatial, policy, and textual data, this project offers a more dynamic view of homelessness in San Diego.
